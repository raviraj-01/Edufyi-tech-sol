FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about applying and partnering with UpFound.

Who is eligible to apply for UpFound?

Any founder working on an early-stage venture from idea to revenue stage across tech verticals such as SaaS, fintech, climate, edtech, and healthtech.

Do I need to be incorporated before applying?

No. You can apply with an idea or prototype. We assist shortlisted founders with legal incorporation and cap table structuring during onboarding.

How many applications can I submit?

Each registered founder account can have up to 3 active applications in review simultaneously.

How long does the evaluation process take?

Our screening committee reviews submissions continuously. You will typically see your application status updated within 5 to 7 business days.

Does UpFound take equity upfront?

No upfront equity is taken. Terms are discussed and aligned on an individual basis only when direct capital investment is finalized.
